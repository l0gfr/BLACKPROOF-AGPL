# BLACKPROOF Board Memo

Version: blackproof-board-memo-v0.1.0-alpha

The Board Memo is the executive summary generated from a ProofPack.

## Purpose

The ProofPack is operational.

The Board Memo is decision-oriented.

It summarizes:

- ProofDebt indicator;
- critical and high proof debt;
- weak, missing, expired or declarative evidence;
- sensitive or non-exportable evidence;
- answer readiness;
- priority remediation actions;
- disclaimer.

## Audience

- founder;
- CEO;
- DSI;
- RSSI;
- board;
- cyber consultant;
- account owner preparing a supplier response.

## Non-promise

The Board Memo is not a certification, legal opinion, qualified audit or regulatory conformity guarantee.

It is a decision-support summary of the BLACKPROOF evidence dossier.
