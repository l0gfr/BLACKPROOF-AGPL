# BLACKPROOF: public product boundary

BLACKPROOF is free software under AGPL-3.0-only. The public website presents one
application, with the same creation, import, encryption, export and verification
features for every user.

Primary audiences: organizations preparing supplier-security answers, security
teams reviewing supporting references, and recipients verifying a Delivery.

The app is local-first and requires no account. It does not receive documents,
answers, evidence contents or encryption keys. Dossiers are not synchronized.
Do not promise certification, legal advice or truth verification.

Public entry points:

- `/start`: choose a workflow;
- `/app`: create a dossier;
- `/questionnaire-import`: read a local questionnaire;
- `/app/cases`: reopen and back up local encrypted dossiers;
- `/verify`: verify an existing artifact locally;
- `/open-source`: licence, corresponding source and self-hosting.

The standalone verifier and schemas share the AGPL licence for original
project content. Third-party materials keep their own notices.
